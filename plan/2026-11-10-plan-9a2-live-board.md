**Goal:** Make 9a-1's plain board page the live board of spec 10.2. A person sees, on a phone first and beside the feed on a wide screen: a header generated by rules (the state and stop reason; the conclusion with its status word, `confirming` included; the budget used; the number of suggestions; the alert's instances and their firing state; the latest time the run stops; when the board last changed); the incident's other items with their conclusions, flagged when they disagree; the hypotheses ranked, each with up to two quotes a side by the spec's fixed rule and its suggestions beneath it, the ruled-out ones after; the feed merging the board's entries with the item's records, platform evidence shown from its typed records and never from a claim string; notices in the platform's words; the board-state table but the two banners 7c-2's fields need; and, for the item's members alone, raw outputs, decoded or as stored, with line numbers and the quote marked. Every stored text is inert and isolated. A same-origin script polls the board's regions every 5 s with a conditional request and replaces only the regions that changed, keeping focus and the reader's place; without it the page is the whole board. Three carries of 9a-1 are taken: a subject with surrounding white space is refused at sign-in, the standby judges the lease by the database's time, and a form refused for its CSRF token after a new sign-in is rendered again with its key.

**Architecture:**
- **Pure first.** `internal/work/board` gains the quote rule over the assessment's own walk, proposals per hypothesis, a contributor's counted sources, the events, and the status words `confirming` and superseded (Task 1). `internal/work/item` gains D5's times and the alert's instances (Task 2).
- **One projection, two parts.** `app.Views.Board` builds an item's `BoardView` from its records and its run's board, cut at a version: everything the records and the board determine, the lifecycle's record facts included (Task 2). `app.BoardWatch` serves the board's reads: one probe a second for every item read in the last 10 s, projections cached by version and built once, the incident's strip from one listing and at most ten folds, and the facts of now (the kill switch, the queue, the auto-close) computed at each read (Task 3).
- **The web's own pool, for the board alone.** Every board read (the board page, its polls, raw outputs, the probe, the strip) goes through a pool of two connections that serve opens beside the coordinator's. Every page 9a-1 shipped outside the board, the kill switch's among them, keeps its path and the main pool as today, so no viewer takes the coordinator's connections and no viewer delays a stop (Task 3; R143).
- **One line model.** Every sentence that holds a stored value is built as typed parts and rendered by one template, each value inert and isolated (R171).
- **The pages.** Raw outputs and the display functions `inertText` and `inertLine` (Task 4); the board page in regions, complete without JavaScript (Tasks 5 and 6); 9a-1's carries (Task 7); the live refresh: `GET /items/{id}/board`, its `ETag` and order, the board pages' policy and `live.<hash>.js` (Task 8); scenarios 33 and 34 and the browser gate's environment (Task 9); the documents (Task 10).
- **The cut line.** The board is complete without its script once Task 7 lands; Task 8 alone may be cut (Decision B).

**Tech Stack:** Go 1.26 (`go 1.26.0`); the standard library's `net/http` and `html/template`; pgx v5.10.0; testify v1.12.1; testcontainers-go v0.44.0; `golang.org/x/net/html` in tests, already a direct requirement; Postgres 17. **No module joins the graph:** the watch's single flights are maps of channels under its lock, so `golang.org/x/sync` stays indirect. The one JavaScript file, `internal/web/pages/static/live.js`, is plain ES2020, embedded and served as written: no build step, no npm, no test runner (R166; Decision A).

## Plan-time rulings

Where the design is silent, ambiguous or infeasible against the code, the smallest decision consistent with the spec and the design. Each is the planner's call; approving the plan approves it. Rulings changed by the plan review say so.

- **R136 (Section 5's cut).** Ten tasks, not the design's eight. The design's Task 2 is split into the projection (Task 2: pure, on the coordinator harness's memory stores) and the watch (Task 3: the probe and the strip's listing in both stores, the caches, the web's pool and serve), which have different test regimes and files; its Task 4 into the board's header, strip and hypotheses (Task 5) and its feed (Task 6). The design's Tasks 2 (3.5–4) and 4 (3.5–4.5) were past the project's ceiling of 3.5 raw days a task (Plan 9a-1's I12). *Cost:* two more briefs and reviews, about 0.25 day.
- **R137 (D2, the tick; plan review M2).** The tick is lazy: a read that finds the latest probe older than 1 s probes every item read in the last 10 s, its own included; one that finds it fresh but without its item probes that item alone and merges it in (re-review N7). One probe runs at a time per instance, so the results only grow. **A probe runs apart from any request** (`context.WithoutCancel`), within 1 s, and each reader waits under its own context, so a reader that gives up fails no other. **A failed probe** leaves the last good tick in use for at most 5 s; past that, until a probe succeeds, a read answers 503 (and at once when no tick ever succeeded). No goroutine ticks, so nothing is left for serve's drain to stop. *Cost:* the first read after an idle second waits on one probe query; a database blip shows a board up to 5 s old; and a page loaded just after a person's own act may come from the tick before it, at most a second old: the act's own answer says what happened, and the page may show the board from before the act until its next poll, within 5 s, or without the script until a reload (added during execution, Task 9).
- **R138 (D2, the version's run).** The probe finds each item's run by its `run_created` record and that run's latest entry in the same statement (Task 3), so the watch keeps no map of runs; an item has one run until Plan 8a, which takes the visible primary run's. *Cost:* Plan 8a changes one join.
- **R139 (D2, the kill switch and the standby; plan review M12(a)).** The tick reads the kill switch's table once on every instance, the leader's included, rather than the coordinator's view: that view is empty until the coordinator's first publish after a takeover (`internal/work/app/queue.go:273-276`), is reset at every fence (`coordinator.go:886-888`), and may be unsettled (R37), and 9a-1's fixed header shows the table (M6). This is the design's own standby path, made the only one. On a standby the board pages keep their lease holder's read for 1 s, a cache of their own; the read itself stays 9a-1's, on the main pool, under its 1 s read timeout (`holderTimeout`, `internal/web/pages/standby.go:8-11`). *Cost:* one more query a second per instance while anyone reads a board.
- **R140 (D2, a projection is its version's).** `Board` builds from the records and the board events cut at the version's seqs (both load in commit order), so a cached projection is exactly its version's, whatever committed since. A record or entry at the version's seq whose id is not the version's (a restore between the probe and the load) fails `ErrMoved`, answered 503, and the next tick probes again. A build that fails for good (invalid records or board, `item.ErrInvalid` or `board.ErrInvalid`) is cached as its error for its version, so a corrupt item costs no read a poll; a transient failure is not cached. *Cost:* a read after a restore answers 503 once.
- **R141 (D2, the pool's wait; plan review I5).** pgxpool v5 has no wait bound of its own on acquiring a connection, so the design's [2 s] bounds the board's whole read: the board page, its polls and the raw outputs read under a 2 s context (`boardWait`), and past it answer 503. A projection's build is shared and runs apart from any one request (`context.WithoutCancel`), bounded by the database's transaction timeout [10 s], so a slow build still lands in the cache for the next poll. **The pages 9a-1 shipped outside the board keep their path, their pool and their behaviour, with no new bound** (R143). *Cost:* a board whose first build takes 2 to 10 s answers 503 once, then is served from the cache.
- **R142 (D2, the web's pool; plan review M9).** `db.PoolOptions` gains `MaxConns` (0 keeps pgx's default). Serve opens a third pool, `server.web`, of 2 connections with `poolOptions`' bounds, only with a `web` block, and closes it with the others. `worstDrain` counts three pools whatever the block, as R114 counts five servers whatever is configured: `9*stopWait`, so the defaults give **1m42s**, not 1m37s (`cmd/agentswarm/serve.go:1040`, `serve_test.go:586`, `serve_integration_test.go:260`, the README and Plan 7d-1's C1 text), and the examples' stale "1m27s" become 1m42s (`examples/two-instances/a.yaml:78`, `b.yaml:70`, `README.md:213`, `agentswarm@.service:29`; `TimeoutStopSec=120` still clears it, `TestTheExampleUnitStopsAfterTheWorstDrain`). *Cost:* a supervisor's stop timeout 5 s higher than needed without a web block; a deployment with raised drain timings checks its supervisor's (Task 10's README note).
- **R143 (D2, which reads use which pool; plan review I5, M7).** The web's pool serves the board's reads alone: the watch's probe, records, events, runs, titles, entries, sibling listings and its tick's kill switch read, and the raw outputs' read calls, all unguarded. **Every page 9a-1 shipped outside the board keeps its path and the main pool as today**: `web.Ports.Views` stays the admin API's views (the items list, the act and opening forms' reads, the kill switch's page, its POST, `UnstaffedBy`), and `web.Ports.LeaseHolder` stays on the main pool (serve.go:588). The health routes' schema checks stay on the main pool, as the admin API's. Revisions: R175. *Cost:* none known.
- **R144 (D3, visibility).** `app.Visible(*View) bool`, true until Plan 8a, is the one predicate: `ViewPorts.Visible` defaults to it and the strip asks it of each shown sibling's view built from its fold; `pages.visible` defaults to it and stays the page's seam that tests swap (`internal/web/pages/board.go:19`). `Board` builds a hidden item's projection as any other's, and the page shows its copy alone (D13); building none is carried to 8a. *Cost:* Plan 8a swaps one function and drops a hidden item's build.

- **R146 (D5, where the times live; plan review M1).** `item.Run.StopBy`, `ConfirmingUntil` and `Confirming` compute D5's times in `internal/work/item`, beside the rules of `decide.go` and `state.go` they mirror. **W is `Grace + SettleWait + SettlePoll + Retry`:** a round's decision lands at the settle's first poll at or after `Grace + SettleWait` (`internal/work/app/rounds.go:317-322`), and a failure waits `Retry`. No T is shown before the run's creation. A time shown as "by T at the latest" or "confirming until T" is rounded up to the minute, so the minute shown is never before T. *Cost:* none known.

- **R150 (D8, trigger evidence's record).** A trigger update's platform evidence is matched to the `item_triggered` of its `Provenance.Source` and the event id after `trigger:` (re-review I8: an event id is unique per source only). One that matches none reads "a trigger event this item's records do not hold", never its claim. *Cost:* none known.
- **R151 (D8, titles).** `Titles` is a narrow port, `app.InboundTitles`, beside `app.Inbound`, which only the views take, as `ClosedLister` is (R92), so the coordinator's ports and their fakes (`harness_test.go`'s `failing`, `interleave_world_test.go`'s `recordsPort`) do not change. *Cost:* one port more.
- **R152 (D10, the gate).** `app.May` gains `ActReadOutput`, decided `Member` alone, never `AdminOverride` (spec 10.1; re-review M7), and the raw-output page shows only when `May` answers `Member`. *Cost:* one act kind; no code.
- **R153 (D10, the rendering; plan review I4).** A raw output that is one JSON document, or the start of one cut by a size limit, is shown as an outline of its decoded tokens: one member or element a line, indented two spaces a level; the top level opens no line; a member as its decoded key, `: `, and its value; a string decoded, without its quotes, its further lines continuing two spaces past its member's indent; a number, a boolean or null as its JSON text; an object or an array as its key's line alone, its members a level in. ```go
// T = D + W + max(0, B − U) + n·W + k·(L + W), an upper bound absent an
// outage, a takeover or a resume, which move T later.
func (r *Run) StopBy(latest time.Time, w Waits) time.Time {
	W, L, b := w.Grace+w.Settle+w.Poll+w.Retry, r.Created.RoundLimit, r.Budget()
	rounds, used := r.usage()                                     // counted normal rounds and their wall clock
	counted, opened := r.wrapUps(&RoundEnded{}), r.wrapUpRounds() // an open wrap-up counted, as no fault ends it
	d := latest
	if l := r.Latest(); l != nil && l.Ended == nil {
		d = l.Opened.Deadline
		if l.Opened.WrapUp == "" { // a wrap-up counts no wall clock (precision 1)
			rounds, used = rounds+1, used+max(d.Sub(l.Opened.OpenedAt), 0)
		}
	}
	n := max(0, b.Rounds-rounds)
	k := max(0, min(MaxWrapUps-counted, MaxWrapUpRounds-opened)) // the open wrap-up out only as it counts (precision 2)
	return d.Add(W + max(0, b.WallClock-used) + time.Duration(n)*W + time.Duration(k)*(L+W))
}

Each statement sees one snapshot. An item's first record is its `item_opened` (the fold's first rule), so `o.seq = 1` reads it on the key. The memory stores answer the same (`memory.NewProbe(records *Records, events *Store)`; `Records.Siblings`), each under its lock.

**The watch's rules:**
- **The tick (R137).** One probe at a time per instance, run apart from any request within 1 s, each reader waiting under its own context. A read whose item the latest result covers, and which is younger than 1 s, uses it; a result older than 1 s is replaced by a probe of every item read in the last 10 s and this one; a fresh result that lacks the item is merged with a probe of that item alone. The same tick reads the kill switch's table once (R139); a failed read sets `KillSwitchRead` false. A failed probe leaves the last good tick in use for 5 s, then reads answer 503 until a probe succeeds.
- **The projections.** Cached by `BoardVersion`, at most 64, the oldest inserted evicted first; one build per version at a time (a map of channels under the watch's lock), run apart from any request's context, bounded by `BuildWait` (R141); a lasting error cached for its version (R140).
- **The strip (design D7; R172).** Per incident, one build at a time, apart from any request, within 2 s: one `Siblings` call (limit 10; its failure, or the bound's: `Unread`, the rest of the board unchanged); each listed item's records folded (on failure, or held by a later build: `Unreadable`); a visible one whose run stopped gets `Conclusion` by one `Entry` read of `RunStopped.Conclusion`, `Converged` when it stopped converged; a hidden one (`Visible` of its view built from its fold) its state alone, with no `Entry` read; `More` is the total less those listed. A good strip is cached for 30 s, a failed one for 5 s; a strip unread for 30 s is dropped. At each read, `Disagrees` is set among the shown visible items and this item's own converged conclusion (R148).
- **The facts (R169).** `QueuePosition` from `Lifecycle().Waiting` while `Held`; the auto-close's pause, time and unread flags from the tick's kill switch through `closesAt`; nothing the records determine.
- **The web's pool (R142, R143).** `server.web` opens with `webPoolOptions(d)`, which is `poolOptions(d)` with `MaxConns` 2, only with a web block. `newWebStores(pool, d)` makes the board's stores unguarded on it: records (with their titles and siblings), events (with their entries), runs, calls, controls (for the tick's read) and the probe. The web's views are `app.NewViews` over those records and boards, with the admin views' `ViewPorts` but the web's stores as `Controls`, `Closed`, `Incidents`, `Titles`, `Entries` and `Siblings`, `Visible` nil, `Waits` from `d.Timing.Coordinator` (`Grace`, `SettleWait`, `SettlePoll`, `Retry`), and `Revisions` the cache-only `cachedRevisions` over the registry (R175). The watch is `NewBoardWatch` over them, `BuildWait` `d.Timing.Database.TransactionTimeout`, and `server` keeps it for the wiring test. `webPorts` takes the watch as `Boards`; `Views` stays the admin API's views and `LeaseHolder` the main pool's (R143). `close` closes the three pools within `stopWait` each.

**`live.js`, as steps** (about 120 lines, at most 150; R166):
1. **Start.** With no `#board`, or no `XMLHttpRequest` or `document.adoptNode`, it does nothing: the page stays reload-only. Otherwise it reads `#board`'s `data-etag` and `data-order` (none for a hidden item) and the `summary` region's `data-round` as the round announced last, sets the indicator to the bar's `data-live`, shows the Pause button, and below 60em closes `#feed-list`.
2. **Poll.** Every 5 s while `document.visibilityState` is `visible`; none while hidden; at once on `visibilitychange` to visible and on `pageshow`. One request in flight; a poll due meanwhile sets a flag that polls again as soon as it ends.
3. **Request.** `GET` of `location.pathname + "/board" + location.search`, `responseType = "document"`, `If-None-Match` the applied `ETag`, a 15 s timeout.
4. **304.** A success; nothing changes.
5. **200.** A response that is no `Document` stops the polling and restores the no-JS note. Its `#board`'s `data-order` with either part below the applied order is dropped (another instance's lag; re-review N3), a success. Otherwise each region is applied (step 6), then the `ETag` and order of the response's `#board` taken (R173).
6. **A region.** A region id missing from the page or from the response reloads the page once (R170, R165's memory). A region whose `data-v` is unchanged is skipped. One that holds `document.activeElement`, or, while updates are paused, any but `board-header`, is kept pending, its update control shown. Otherwise it is replaced: each `<details>`' `open` state copied by id from the old to the new, the first region on screen and its offset noted, `old.replaceWith(document.adoptNode(fresh))`, focus restored by id when that element is in the new region, and the noted region scrolled back to its offset.
7. **Pending.** Applied when focus leaves its region and updates are not paused, or when its control is activated, focus then on the region itself.
8. **Pause.** Toggles `aria-pressed`; un-pausing applies every pending region that does not hold focus.
9. **The polite summary.** When the new `summary` region's `data-round` differs from the round announced last, its text goes to `#announce` by `textContent`, and that round becomes the last announced (plan review M10(g)).
10. **Errors.** Status 0, a timeout, or any status but 200, 304 and 401: the next poll after 10, 20, 40, then 60 s, back to 5 s after a success. The indicator reads `data-live` while the last success is within 10 s, else the header region's `data-stale`.
11. **401.** If `sessionStorage` holds no reload in the last 60 s, it notes one and calls `location.reload()`; otherwise, or when the storage throws (R165), it stops polling and shows the "Sign in again" link.
12. **Never throws (R170).** Each handler's body runs under a guard: a fault reads as an error of step 10, so the bar reads "reconnecting", never "live", and the next poll is scheduled.
13. **No sink.** It uses the XHR document, `adoptNode`, `replaceWith`, `textContent`, `hidden`, `open`, `dataset`, `setAttribute("aria-pressed", …)`, `focus` and `scrollBy`, and nothing of the Global Constraints' list; it holds no word.

## Review Focus

The design says what each rule must do, not every input it meets. These five are the inputs most likely to bite that the design's tests would not reach; each has its test in the task that owns the code.

1. **Hostile text through every real path** (plan review I3). The test writes an item's records and its board's events straight to the memory stores, past the gateway's and the coordinator's input checks, as a later build or a corrupt row could, and reads them through the real `app.Views` and `BoardWatch` behind the wired web server. Each path gets its own tagged value, `<script>x</script>` + U+202E + `tag-<n>` + U+200B + a line feed: every record kind's stored fields (a title, routing keys and values, `By`, a reason's text, an instance id, an alert's title through `Titles`), every board entry kind's (an agent's and a person's names and claims, a quote, a source, a statement, a task's title and tags, a ruling's reason, a proposer, summary and fact values), every notice's names (agent, tool, rule, skill), a strip sibling's state words and key, a raw output and its arguments, the layout's switch `By`, reason and leader name, and the items list's title, service, principal and responders. On the board page, its regions document, the raw output's page and the items list, for each tag: the body holds no raw U+202E or U+200B anywhere; `⟨U+202E⟩tag-<n>⟨U+200B⟩` appears, each time inside a `<bdi>`; a single-line path shows its line feed as `⟨U+000A⟩`; no `script` element but `live.js`'s, no `on*` attribute, no `href` or `src` starting `javascript:`. A path that skips `inertText`, `inertLine` or `<bdi>` fails its row. (`TestAHostileStringThroughEveryPathRendersInert`, Task 6, `cmd/agentswarm/board_render_test.go`)
2. **Platform words spoofed by stored text.** An alert title "Alert resolved; service=payments", a routing value `checkout, region=eu-1`, a claim "Received late, not applied", a statement "converged", a reason "Stopped by the kill switch": each appears only inside `<q class="data">`, after "model-written", or after "a person's words", and the page's platform words appear exactly as often as the records and the board warrant. (`TestStoredTextThatReadsAsPlatformWordsNeverReadsAsThem`, Task 6)
3. **A stale or out-of-order poll.** A takeover between two polls reaches an instance whose tick lags: its answer must carry an order below the page's, which the script drops, and one watch must never answer an older version than it gave. (`TestAWatchNeverAnswersAnOlderVersionThanItGaveBefore`, Task 3; `TestALaggingInstancesPollCarriesTheLowerOrder`, Task 8, two watches over one store, one's probe held a tick behind)
4. **An expired session or a rotated key mid-poll.** A poll with an expired session, with a cookie sealed under a rotated key, with none, and while the key cannot be read: `401` with no body or `503`, never a `Location`, never a `Set-Cookie`, and the IdP never asked, so no flow cookie churns (review I1.4); the script itself loads with no session. (`TestABoardPollWithoutALiveSessionAnswers401AndNeverStartsASignIn`, Task 8)
5. **A hidden item's leak.** With the seam saying no, the page, its polls and its raw outputs hold no `X-Board-Order`, no `data-order`, no "last updated", no round number, no time or seq of its run; the `ETag` is unchanged while the hidden run gains rounds, entries and records, and changes when the kill switch turns; every call id answers 404; and its sibling's strip shows its state alone (D13). (`TestAHiddenItemsBoardTellsNothingOfItsRun`, Task 8; `TestAHiddenSiblingShowsItsStateAloneAndNothingOfItsRunIsRead`, Task 3)
