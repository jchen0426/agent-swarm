## 1. How the work is split

Phase 0 was estimated at 19–25 person-weeks (spec Section 14.2), re-estimated on 2026-11-01 at about 37–48, and corrected on 2026-11-02 to about 46–54 (Section 2). One plan for all of it would go stale before half of it ran, and two of its early steps can stop the project (spec Section 14.1). So the work is split into plans. Each plan leaves working, tested software, and each is written in detail only when its turn comes, from what the earlier plans taught.

Plan 1 is written in full. The other plans are outlined below.

**How estimates are kept.** Each plan has two figures in Section 2's table. Its size, in person-weeks on spec Section 14.2's yardstick, "an experienced engineer making heavy use of AI coding assistants", measures the work. Its calendar time, a guess of the days of building at the pace the record shows, dates it: Plan 7d set that pace at about 0.55–0.7 person-weeks a day, designs and reviews included, and the guess leaves out time spent waiting for the author's decisions. Each plan's design sizes its plan, and the table records that size beside the plan's estimate. A design, or an execution, that comes to more than one and a half times the estimate is a revisit trigger of ADR-016, and the plans after it are re-estimated. A split re-estimates both parts. When a plan is executed, the table gives its size as executed, made from what it delivered, and its dates.

## 4. Implementation decisions

These apply to every plan. Each plan may refine them, and says so.

- **Module and binary.** The module is `github.com/jchen0426/agent-swarm`. There is one binary, `agentswarm`, with subcommands; Plan 7d-1 added `serve`, `migrate`, `turn` and `inspect`.
- **Bounded contexts.** The contexts of spec Section 2.2 are confirmed: Work and Actions (core), Registry, Measurement and LLM (supporting), Access and Audit (generic). Plan 6b added LLM.
- **Layout.** Each context lives under `internal/<context>/`, with:
  - domain packages named after their concept (`internal/work/key`, `internal/work/board`);
  - one `app` package for its use cases and the ports they declare (`internal/work/app`);
  - one package per adapter technology (`internal/work/postgres`).

  Shared infrastructure sits in `internal/db` (connection and migrations) and `internal/ids` (identifiers). Wiring lives in `cmd/agentswarm`. Black-box tests of the built binary live in `test/e2e`.
- **Database.** One Postgres database with one migration sequence, embedded in the binary. Migrations are expand-only, and each declares the oldest build that can read the schema it leaves, its `min_reader` (Plan 7d-1). Tests run against Postgres 17 in containers, and every container a test starts carries the label `agentswarm.project=agent-swarm`, by which `make test-clean` finds those a killed run left.
- **Libraries.** pgx v5 for Postgres. River for jobs that must outlive the records, from Plan 9 (ADR-004). cel-go and an RFC 9535 JSONPath library, from Plan 4 (ADR-015). The official MCP Go SDK, from Plan 5. No web framework: the standard library's HTTP server.
